Why HyPair exists

Built by a HYROX gym member.

I train at a HYROX gym, and I kept seeing the same thing. Whenever a SIM or an official HYROX or TRYKA race came up, plenty of people wanted to join but didn't know who to ask. Some were too shy. Some worried they weren't good enough. And some said yes to whoever asked first, then found their partner was at a very different level.

I built HyPair so finding the right partner isn't left to chance, and the best part of race day is the race itself.

Ante, founder of HyPair

FAQ

Common questions

Will there be athletes at my event? +

HyPair is new, so it depends on the event. Right now 26 upcoming HYROX and TRYKA races are listed across 23 cities in 7 countries, and the number of athletes grows as more people join. Once you sign up for an event, you appear in the list of athletes looking for a partner, so others can find you, and HyPair can let you know when more athletes join. Pace is self-reported, and any profile can be reported or blocked.

What is HyPair? +

HyPair is a partner-matching app for HYROX and TRYKA doubles athletes. It matches you by pace, finish goal and the exact race you're entering, so you find the right partner, not just whoever's available.

Which events does HyPair support? +

HyPair supports HYROX doubles and TRYKA doubles, for official events and gym simulation events. We're live across Ireland, the UK, Europe, the US, Canada, Australia and South Africa. If your event or city isn't listed yet, get in touch and let us know.

How does the matching algorithm work? +

HyPair scores compatibility from three things: running pace, race format and division, and finish goal. The closer the match on all three, the higher the compatibility score on each profile.

Is HyPair free to use? +

Yes, HyPair is free for athletes, and we'll be upfront if that ever changes. Gyms get their first event free; see pricing for gyms.

Is HyPair available now? +

Yes, HyPair is live on the App Store and Google Play, so you can download it and start matching today. Not in a supported country yet? Let us know and we'll factor it into where we expand next.

Can I find a partner for a gym SIM? +

Yes. HyPair covers gym simulation events as well as official HYROX and TRYKA races. Browse athletes entered for your SIM, check their pace and goal, and connect.

Can I find a partner if I'm a beginner? +

Yes. Not everyone is chasing a PB. Every profile shows pace and goal, so you can find someone racing for the same reasons you are, whether that's a fast time or a first doubles race with good company.

What happens when I find someone I want to race with? +

Send a request and chat. When you both agree to race, you lock it in and you're paired. From there you can compare your paces and make a plan.

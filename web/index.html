FAQ

Common questions

What is HyPair? +

HyPair is a partner-matching app for hybrid race athletes. It pairs you with a doubles partner for HYROX and Tryka by 5K pace and finish goal. Matching for gym-run SIM events is coming soon.

Which events does HyPair support? +

HyPair supports HYROX doubles and Tryka doubles today, with gym-run SIM events coming soon. We're starting in Ireland and the UK and expanding from there — if your event or city isn't listed yet, get in touch and let us know.

How does the matching algorithm work? +

HyPair scores compatibility based on three factors: 5K training pace (within 3% is ideal), race format and division, and finish goal alignment. The closer the match on all three, the higher the compatibility score shown on each profile.

Is HyPair free to use? +

Yes — HyPair is free for athletes. We'll be transparent if that changes. Gym owners will have a free tier to list events, with optional premium tools for managing larger events.

Is HyPair available now? +

Yes — HyPair is live on the App Store and Google Play now. Download it and start matching today, whichever HYROX or Tryka event you're entering.

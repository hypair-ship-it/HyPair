FAQ

Common questions

What is HyPair? +

HyPair is a partner-matching app for hybrid race athletes. It pairs you with a doubles partner for HYROX, Tryka and in-gym SIM events — matched by 5K pace, finish goal and the exact race you're entering.

Which events does HyPair support? +

HyPair supports HYROX doubles, Tryka doubles, and gym-run SIM events. We're starting in Ireland and expanding from there — if your event or city isn't listed yet, join the waitlist and let us know.

How does the matching algorithm work? +

HyPair scores compatibility based on three factors: 5K training pace (within 5 sec/km is ideal), race format and division, and finish goal alignment. The closer the match on all three, the higher the compatibility score shown on each profile.

Is HyPair free to use? +

Yes — HyPair is free for athletes during beta. We'll be transparent if that changes. Gym owners will have a free tier to list events, with optional premium tools for managing larger events.

When does HyPair launch? +

We're in beta now — join the waitlist to be among the first users. TRYKA Dublin at the RDS runs 3–5 July, and HYROX Dublin at the RDS runs 11–15 November 2026. We're building so you're paired before both.

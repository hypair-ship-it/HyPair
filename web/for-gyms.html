Why gyms use HyPair

A great experience for your athletes.

Many athletes would love to enter a SIM or an official race but don't know who to ask. Some are too shy, some worry they're not good enough, and some say yes to whoever asked first and end up with a partner who slows them down. HyPair fixes that for your members.

  • More members take part. They can find a partner in the app without asking around the group chat.
  • Partners who fit. Athletes are matched by pace and goal, so nobody is stuck with a partner who doesn't suit them.
  • New pairings in your own gym. Members of a similar level can meet people they would never have thought to ask.
  • Get your SIM in front of more athletes. Travellers and local competitors can find your SIM in the app, match with a partner and race with you. Some may end up joining your gym.
  • Free for athletes. There's no cost to your members for finding a partner.

How it works

Everything in one place.

1

Set up your event

2

Athletes sign up and pair up

Your event is visible to athletes in the app. They sign up, find a partner by pace and goal, and can see what's happening.

3

Manage it in one place

See who's paired and who's still solo, add members who aren't on the app, build the waves and share the times. Everyone sees the same plan.

Questions

Common questions from gyms.

How do I list my gym's SIM event?

Use the "Get in touch" button or email us, and we'll help you set up your first event. You add the date, formats and slots in the app.

Can athletes outside my gym join?

Yes. Your event is visible to athletes using HyPair, so travellers and local competitors can find it, sign up and look for a partner.

How does partner matching work for my event?

Athletes who sign up can browse each other by pace, goal and age group and agree to pair up in the app. You can see who's paired and who's still looking, and add members who aren't on the app.

What about members who aren't on HyPair?

Add them by hand and they sit in the same plan as everyone else. You can also change pairs yourself whenever you like.

How do my athletes find out their wave time?

Once your waves are built, you share the times with everyone who signed up.

Do I need to manage the matching myself?

No. Athletes find their own partners. You can step in and pair people by hand whenever you like.

How much does it cost?

Your first event is free. After that you pay per event, based on how many athletes take part (see the pricing above). HyPair is free for athletes.

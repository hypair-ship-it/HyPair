How it works

Three steps.

1

Set up your event

Add your gym and your SIM in the app: date, formats and slots. We help you with the first one.

2

Athletes sign up and pair up

Athletes find a partner by pace and goal, in the app. You can also add members by hand.

3

Run the day

Waves are built for you and the schedule goes to your members on WhatsApp.

For every gym

  • Pay per event, by size. No subscription, no contract. Your first event is free.
  • You pay in the app when you publish an event, after your free events. There is no invoice. Payments in the app are being set up; your free events don't need it.
  • Prices in euros. VAT included where it applies. Founding prices last 24 months from approval, kept while the gym runs at least one event every 12 months.

  • Your first 3 SIM events free, any size

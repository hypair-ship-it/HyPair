What we ask in return

Fair both ways.

Not for you after your first event? Tell us why in a 5-minute chat, and we won't contact you again. No hard feelings.

And one more promise: if your event doesn't run smoothly on HyPair, your next one is free.

Founding Gym terms

The plain-language terms a gym accepts when it applies as a Founding Gym.

What you get

  1. Your first 3 SIM events on HyPair free, any size.
  2. For 24 months from the day we accept you as a Founding Gym: EUR 25 per event up to 40 athletes, EUR 45 up to 100 athletes. Over 100: we agree a price together. Your 3 free events are used first. The founding price stays as long as you run at least one event on HyPair every 12 months.
  3. A founder helps set up your first event, and we answer problems the same day. If your event doesn't run smoothly on HyPair, your next one is free.
  4. A Founding Gym badge on your gym profile in the app and a listing on hypair.app.
  5. A say in what we build next.

What we ask

  1. A 15-minute feedback chat after each event.
  2. Permission to use your gym's name, logo and photos from your HyPair events in HyPair's posts, website and app store pages. We only use photos of athletes who agreed to be shown. You can ask us to remove any photo.
  3. A short quote from you, only if you want to give one. We never write one for you.

Not for you?

After your first event you can tell us it is not for you. We will ask for a 5-minute chat with three questions (what didn't work, what would have made you stay, would you consider HyPair again later) and then we will not contact you again. We stop featuring your gym in new posts. Posts already published stay up unless you ask us to take them down.

Good to know

  • Places are limited to the first 10 gyms. A HyPair founder approves each application.
  • You run your event and are responsible for it, including your insurance. HyPair is the app you use to manage sign-ups, partners and waves.
  • HyPair is independent of HYROX. Please call your event a "HYROX-style SIM".
  • How we handle athlete data is in our Privacy policy.
  • You can leave at any time, and so can we, with notice by message or email to joinhypair@gmail.com.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 May 2026

HyPair is a mobile app that helps athletes find doubles partners for hybrid races. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it. If you have questions, email us at hello@hypair.app.

1. Who we are

HyPair is operated by HyPair Ltd (Ireland). When this policy refers to "HyPair", "we", "us" or "our", it means HyPair Ltd.

For the purposes of GDPR, HyPair Ltd is the data controller for personal data collected through the HyPair app and website.

2. What data we collect

Account data — when you sign up, we collect your email address and a password (stored as a secure hash, never in plain text).

Profile data — to help you find race partners, you can optionally provide: first and last name, date of birth, gender, city, country, profile photo, bio, training pace, race history and race goals.

Event data — which races you've registered for, your format (Solo / Doubles / Mixed Doubles), and your wave start time.

Matching data — partner requests you've sent or received, chat messages exchanged with other athletes, and pairing confirmations.

Usage data — basic app analytics (screens visited, errors) to help us fix bugs. We do not sell this data.

Push notification token — if you grant permission, we store a device token to send you match and chat notifications. You can revoke this at any time in your device settings.

3. Why we collect it

  • To match you with compatible race partners based on pace, format and event
  • To enable in-app chat between matched athletes
  • To send you notifications about partner requests and messages
  • To display your public profile to other athletes browsing for partners
  • To improve the app and fix bugs

Our legal basis under GDPR is contract performance (providing the service you signed up for) and legitimate interests (improving the app, preventing abuse).

4. What we share

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it with advertisers.

We share data only with:

  • Supabase (our database and authentication provider, hosted in the EU) — processes data on our behalf under a Data Processing Agreement
  • Expo / EAS — for app distribution and push notification delivery
  • Other app users — your public profile (name, photo, bio, pace, race history) is visible to other authenticated athletes browsing for partners. Your email address is never shared with other users.

5. Your profile visibility

Your profile is visible to other authenticated HyPair users when they browse for partners at events you've registered for. Only registered, signed-in users can see profiles — unauthenticated visitors cannot access any profile data.

You can control what appears on your profile by editing it in the app. Deleting your account removes your profile from partner search immediately.

6. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your profile, event registrations, chat messages and partner requests within 30 days.

We may retain anonymised, aggregated usage data (e.g. how many athletes registered for a race type) indefinitely for analytics purposes.

7. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EEA or UK, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data (you can do most of this directly in the app)
  • Erasure — request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Restriction — request that we limit how we use your data

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@hypair.app. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies

The HyPair website (hypair.app) uses only essential cookies required for the page to function (e.g. Cloudflare security). We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

9. Children

HyPair is not intended for users under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. During sign-up, users must confirm they are 16 or over. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

10. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed using industry-standard bcrypt. We use Row Level Security (RLS) in our database so users can only access their own data and the public profiles of other athletes. We conduct regular security reviews.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. We'll notify active users of material changes via email or an in-app notice. The "last updated" date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: hello@hypair.app

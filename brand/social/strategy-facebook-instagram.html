Instagram

04

Post Formats

Instagram

Reels (priority): Instagram's algorithm gives Reels the most reach. Aim for 15–30 seconds. Hook in first 2 seconds. Use trending audio when relevant to the niche. Post 2× per week.

Carousels: Best for educational or list content ("5 things…", "How to…"). Saves and shares boost algorithmic reach. Post 1–2× per week.

Stories: Daily or near-daily. Polls, countdowns, swipe-up link to waitlist, behind-the-scenes. Stories don't affect reach but keep existing followers warm.

Static posts: Use sparingly — mainly for brand milestones (launch, waitlist numbers) where the visual itself is the message.

Facebook

Group posts: The highest-value Facebook activity. Engage in HYROX UK Community and Spartan groups. Don't post ads — join conversations, answer questions, be genuinely useful. Mention HyPair only when it's directly relevant.

Page posts: 3× per week. Text-heavy posts work on Facebook in a way they don't on Instagram. Longer captions, questions that invite comments, share Instagram Reels here too (Facebook cross-posts Reels natively).

Facebook Events: Create a Facebook Event for every major race on the calendar. Athletes searching for race events will find HyPair this way.

Format tip

Create your Reels for Instagram first, then share them to Facebook. Don't post TikTok-watermarked content — Facebook suppresses it. Keep captions different on each platform — Facebook allows longer, more conversational copy.

Use 8–12 hashtags per Instagram post. Mix core (always use), niche (event or topic specific), and rotating (swap weekly). Facebook: use 3–5 hashtags max — fewer works better there.

06

Facebook Group Strategy

The highest-intent audience is already gathering in these groups. The approach is: listen first, add value second, introduce HyPair third. Never spam.


Group Week 1–2 Week 3–4 Approach
HYROX Official Community Observe Engage Like and comment on posts, answer training questions, never mention the app until Week 3+
HYROX UK Community Engage Mention app Most relevant UK group. When someone posts "looking for a doubles partner" — this is your moment. Reply helpfully, then mention HyPair is coming.
Spartan UK & Ireland (183K likes) Observe Engage Spartan athletes are potential future users. Build presence here without hard selling. Focus on Spartan Beast / Trifecta content.
HyPair Community Group (create) — Launch Week 3 Create your own group in Week 3. Position it as the home for hybrid race partner matching. Invite early waitlist sign-ups. Seed with good content before inviting others.

Golden rule for group posting

The moment someone posts "Does anyone know a good doubles partner for Manchester HYROX?" — that is your highest-value organic moment. Reply immediately, be helpful, and mention HyPair is launching. One well-timed comment in a live group thread is worth more than ten page posts.

  • Set up notifications for the HYROX UK Community group
  • Respond within 30 minutes to partner-matching posts
  • Never copy-paste the same comment — group admins will ban you

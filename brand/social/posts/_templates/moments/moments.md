# Three posting moments per event (group posts)

Agreed 2 Oct 2026. Each event gets up to three group posts, one per moment, each with a different text and angle, at least 10-14 days apart.
Never repost the same thing in a group, and comment on real "need a partner" posts in between. If a group removes a post, stop posting there and only comment.

Rules that always apply: no Tryka in US/Canada copy, no "5K", no arrow on buttons, no gym offer or pricing,
never dating framing, Canada is paused (AthleteField). Screens are from the staging build with sandbox demo profiles.

| Moment | When | Kind of post | Asset |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. On sale | Tickets go on sale or the event is announced | Partner thread (community post, link in the first comment) | None needed. Optional text-led graphic: `make_moment1.py` |
| 2. Mid-countdown | About 3 weeks before | "Partner dropped out?" | Carousel slide with a fresh Find a Partner screenshot |
| 3. Final week | 6-7 days before | Last-minute partner | Boston carousel pattern (4 slides) |

## Text templates (replace {city}, {dates})

**1. Partner thread (post)**
HYROX {city} ({dates}) partner thread 🧵
Got your ticket and still looking for a doubles partner? Drop a comment with:
- Your division (women's, men's or mixed)
- Your usual running pace
- Your goal (chasing a PB, or just here for a good race)
- Where you are based
Easier to find your match here than to keep asking around. 💪

**1. Partner thread (first comment, posted by HyPair)**
Prefer to be matched by pace and goal for this exact race? HyPair does that, free to download: https://hypair.app

**2. Partner dropped out?**
Partner dropped out? It happens, and usually closer to race day than anyone plans for. Racing HYROX {city} ({dates}) and need a partner? HyPair matches you by pace and goal with athletes entering the same event. Free to download: https://hypair.app

**3. Final week**
Racing HYROX {city} ({dates}) and still need a doubles partner? We built HyPair to solve exactly this. It matches you by pace and goal, not just whoever happens to ask first. Free to download: https://hypair.app Good luck out there 💪

(Mention Tryka only for Tryka events in Ireland and the UK. US and Canada copy never does.)

## What to record for every post
Event, moment (1/2/3), group, date and time posted (ET for US events), views, reactions, comments, and the sign-ups for that event in the app
the following 7 days (count real sign-ups only). After 3-4 events, compare the moments and keep the best.

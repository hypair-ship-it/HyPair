Race Selection
Removed from onboarding. Moved to Edit Profile under "Races I do" — feels natural as a preference, not a gate.
Stats Language
Replaced "PRs" and "help partners find" with "your latest performance" and a friendly explanation. Less jargon.
Bottom Nav
Three tabs: Today (dashboard), Races (your events), Profile (stats + settings). Active tab = gold dot indicator.
What's Next
Partner browse / swipe lives inside the Races tab — tap a race → see matches for that event. Contextual, not generic.

## 1. Our position (working draft)

**Core:** event-specific partner matching for HYROX and Tryka (official events and gym simulation events), by pace, finish goal, race format and the specific event entered.

**Where we differ from how people do it today** (Facebook groups, WhatsApp threads, asking at the gym):
- Matching on compatibility, not "whoever asked first".
- Tied to the specific event, so both people are actually racing the same day.
- Covers sim events at gyms, which the big event sites and groups mostly ignore.

**Angle we like most: SIM / GYM.** Gyms bring their members, so HyPair becomes the default place to find a partner for that gym's events. This is also where local density comes from, and density is the real moat (features are easy to copy; a pool of athletes in the same event is not).

**Coming, still being worked on: race style analysis.** Offer athletes (and partners) an analysis of how they race, so matching and prep go beyond a pace number. Scope and wording not decided.

**Honest weak spots**
- Liquidity: a match app is only as good as the number of athletes in the same event.
- Easy to copy: the idea alone isn't defensible.

**What this changes**
1. The idea is no longer unique. ROXMATCH (Sep 2026) does event-first, pace-aware matching, which is our core pitch. At least three apps now chase the same athletes.
2. Event-specific matching alone isn't our edge. What we have that none of the three listings mention: **Tryka** and **gym sim events**, plus gym-run events with organisers in the app. That makes the SIM/GYM angle more important, not less.
3. Race style analysis, if we ship it, goes beyond what these listings show. RoxConnect shows station strengths and results, but we found no analysis of how a pair will race together.
4. Trust features are becoming normal: selfie verification (ROXMATCH), verified results (RoxConnect). Check what we do and whether we need to say it more clearly.
5. Monetisation signals: RoxConnect already has a premium tier; ROXMATCH has founder pricing. We should know our own answer before gyms or athletes ask.
6. Several apps released in the last few months, so the category is heating up and early density matters.

### Flowtrip, flowtrip.app (adjacent)
Source: their homepage, read 16 Oct 2026. Nothing below is from using the app.
- **What it is:** group trip planner (FlowTrip Inc.), iOS and Android, free to get. Replaces spreadsheets and IOU tracking.
- **Who it's for:** friend groups who already know each other (festivals, stag/hen parties, ski trips, golf, cycling, hiking, sports events such as World Cup 2026).
- **Features:** shared itinerary, polls, in-app chat, map view, expense tracker with fair splitting, packing list, notifications nudging people to vote or confirm, AI "Trip Idea Check" that sanity-checks a plan against budget, weather and logistics, plus AI suggestions for hotels, restaurants and activities.
- **Matching / discovery:** none. No public feed or network. Community is inside each group.
- **Tone:** casual, upbeat, "#fun not #stressful", "set up in 60 seconds".
- **Growth:** SEO guide pages per trip type and a blog; TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn.
- **Gaps seen on the page:** no testimonials, user counts or pricing; some screenshots look like placeholders.
- **How we differ:** they start from a group that already exists; we start from strangers who need a partner. Different problem, same moment (a big event with logistics around it).

#### What we can learn from Flowtrip
1. **A "reality check" hook.** Their AI that tests an idea against reality is a clear, playful promise. Our version could come out of race style analysis: "does this pairing / this goal hold up?"
2. **After the match, the trip.** Once two people are paired for an event there is a real logistics tail: travel, accommodation, who brings what, race-day plan. Even a light version (shared checklist, split costs, a simple plan) would keep pairs in the app after matching.
3. **Nudges that get a group to decide.** Their notifications push people to vote or confirm. Same pattern could push a new pair to confirm, say hello, or agree a race-day plan.
4. **One-line simplicity.** "Set up in about 60 seconds" is easy to repeat. Worth testing a similar plain claim for HyPair once we can back it up.
5. **SEO through guides.** They win search with niche guides. Caution: we retired per-event landing pages on 4 Sep 2026 because they don't scale, and the page set is frozen. If we want this, it needs a decision first, probably guides that aren't tied to a single event.
6. **Don't copy:** the "all tools in one app" sprawl. Our focus on finding the right person is the point.

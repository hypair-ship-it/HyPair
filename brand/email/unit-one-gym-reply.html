Thank you for your interest in HyPair! Here are the details.

HyPair matches HYROX and Tryka athletes with a doubles partner by pace and goal, and lets gyms list their sim events so members can pair up before the day.

How to get started

1.Download the app from the App Store or Google Play (buttons below).
2.Create your profile with this email address. It takes about 2 minutes.
3.Tap “I run a gym” and fill in the short request. I’ll approve it as soon as I see it, and you’ll get an email when it’s done.
4.Create your next sim and tap Publish when you’re ready. You can then share it with your members using the QR code card.

What would you need from us to try it at your next sim? We’re happy to help with setup and to answer any questions or suggestions. We’re a small business, so all feedback matters, and even if it isn’t for you, we’d really appreciate knowing why. No pressure from us, just a fair try.

I’ve also attached a ready-to-post image you can share with your members on Instagram, Facebook or WhatsApp, whenever you’d like to spread the word about your next sim.
